Mission

Why are robots built the way they are? At the FORGE Lab, we study how a robot's shape, structure, materials, and movement determine what it can do. We build robots to test our ideas, learn from how they interact with the real world, and uncover principles that connect a robot’s design to its abilities. Our long-term goal is to make robot design less dependent on trial and error—so that one day, we can start with a task and systematically determine what kind of robot to build to accomplish it.
