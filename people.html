Principal Investigator

Wei-Hsi Chen

Assistant Professor
Electrical & Computer Engineering, NC State University

Wei-Hsi Chen directs FORGE Lab, where he studies how a robot's geometry determines what it is able to do. His work spans the algorithmic design of origami mechanisms, leg-wheel transformable and legged robots, and tunable-stiffness actuation — including Kinegami, a method for designing compliant kinematic chains from tubular origami.

Before joining NC State he was a postdoctoral associate at Penn. He received his Ph.D. in Electrical and Systems Engineering and his M.S. in Robotics from the University of Pennsylvania, and his B.S. and M.S. in Mechanical Engineering from National Taiwan University.
